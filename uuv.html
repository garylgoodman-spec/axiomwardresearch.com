UUV RESEARCH PROGRAM

Experimental underwater systems from bench-top control to autonomy.

The UUV program investigates propulsion, control, stabilization, sensing, telemetry and autonomous operation through progressively more capable unmanned underwater vehicle prototypes.

PROPULSIONIMUCONTROLTELEMETRYAUTONOMY

Propulsion & Control

Motor control, thrust management and control-surface actuation.

Stabilization

IMU-informed feedback for attitude sensing and progressively closed-loop control.

Navigation & Telemetry

Architecture for command, state reporting, mission data and future autonomous behaviors.

DEVELOPMENT PATH

Increase complexity only after the fundamentals work.

Bench-top subsystem testing

Validate motors, drivers, controls, sensors and software independently.

Integrated prototype

Combine propulsion, control surfaces and sensing into a test vehicle.

Telemetry & stabilization

Add feedback, logging and communications for controlled trials.

Progressive autonomy

Introduce mission logic and autonomous behaviors after reliable manual operation.

Independent applied research, engineering and prototype development.
